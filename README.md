AgriConnect: AI-Driven Direct-to-Market Web Platform 🌾
Smart India Hackathon (SIH) Project

Problem Statement
Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers' earnings and increase consumer prices.

Our Solution
A digital marketplace that connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers, providing logistics support, AI demand forecasting, and route optimization.


Features
 Digital Marketplace (Direct Buyer-Seller connection)
 AI Demand Forecasting
 AI Route Optimization for Logistics
 Multilingual & Voice-Enabled Interface
 WhatsApp Bot Integration
 AI Crop Quality Grading
